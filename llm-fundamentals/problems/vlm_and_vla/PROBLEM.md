# Vision, language, and action

An interviewer asks: "How does a vision-language model connect a vision encoder to a language model? And in a vision-language-action model, how do discrete action tokens differ from a flow-matching action head?"

Discrete action tokens are the approach used by models such as RT-2 and OpenVLA. A flow-matching action head is the approach used by π0. You do not need parameter counts or benchmark scores.

Answer as you would out loud.
