# Design a multi-tenant fine-tuned model platform

*"We let business customers fine-tune an 8B open model on their own data. Design the serving system. We expect 500 customers, most with little traffic, a few with a lot."* Cover how models are stored and loaded, how requests are batched, how you keep latency fair across customers, how you price or account for cost, and how you keep customers' data and models isolated.
